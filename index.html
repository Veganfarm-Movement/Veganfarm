About VEGANFARM

Turning concern into meaningful action.

VEGANFARM is an independent animal and human welfare movement established in 2025 by Harshit Dedhia.

Our work currently focuses on two areas: supporting animal rescuers across India through funding assistance, and providing free online English education to students in aashrams in Mumbai and Virar.

We aim to build practical support systems that connect people who want to help with genuine welfare and educational needs.

Harshit Dedhia Founder · VEGANFARM · Established 2025
What We Do

Two projects. One purpose.

VEGANFARM currently focuses its work on animal rescue support and accessible education.

🐾

Rescuer Help

We support genuine animal rescuers across India through funding assistance for rescue-related needs.

  • Medical treatment support
  • Animal food requirements
  • Shelter-related needs
  • Emergency rescue expenses
  • Other genuine welfare requirements
Contact VEGANFARM
✦

Aashram Teaching

We provide free online English classes to students in aashrams in Mumbai and Virar.

  • Free English education for students
  • Online classes
  • Programs for aashram students
  • Teacher support through available funding
  • Focus on practical language development
